Hammer of Clarity

Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · updated 21 November 2026 for version 1.0.2

Hammer of Clarity is a browser extension that blocks the websites you choose. It runs entirely on your device. It has no account, no server and no analytics, and it does not collect, sell or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

The sites and categories you block, the reasons you write, your "do this instead" suggestions, your settings, your progress, and a short log of visits to the block page (kept for 30 days and used only to decide when a category has earned trust). All of this is saved in your browser's local extension storage and never leaves your device. Removing the extension deletes it.

How web pages are handled

To block a site, the extension uses Chrome's built-in blocking rules, which compare the address of each page you open with your list on your device. Page addresses and content are not recorded or sent anywhere.

The only network requests

If "Keep the adult and gambling lists up to date" is on (the default), the extension downloads the latest public block lists about once a week from their maintainers:

  • oisd.nl (adult list)
  • raw.githubusercontent.com (HaGeZi gambling list)

These are plain downloads. They send nothing about you, your lists or your browsing, beyond what any download reveals to the server (such as your IP address). Turn the setting off to make no network requests at all.

Permissions

The extension asks for access to all websites only because Chrome requires it to send a blocked page to the extension's own block page, and to read the address of the page you are on when you click "Hammer this site". It does not read or change page content.

From version 1.0.2, when you block a site, the extension removes that site's service worker and cached pages from your browser, so the site can't keep showing pages from its cache. It removes nothing else: no cookies, logins or history, and nothing from sites you haven't blocked. To check whether a page you open should be blocked, it compares the page's address with your lists on your device; addresses are not stored or sent anywhere.

Children

The extension collects no data from anyone, including children.
